What it does

Goes in first.
Comes back with answers.

Old forts, caves and tombs can collapse, and the air inside can be toxic. Archaeologists walk in anyway. Blackout scouts ahead and tells them what's inside.

01Explores

Rocker wheels climb rubble. Lights and camera see in the dark.

02Senses danger

Checks gas, heat, humidity and walls, then says: clear, caution or danger.

03Documents finds

Its AI, Sage, spots rock art or pottery and saves a photo with location.

Built by hand

Four robots in five months.

Every part 3D-printed, wired and tested by us. Each version fixed what broke in the last one.

V1 · May

White plastic frame. Proved the idea works.

V2 · July

Camera, off-road tyres. Sage, our AI, is born.

V3 · September

Springs, first arm. Won the National Final.

V4 · Now

Gripper, 360° scanner, GPS, gas sensor, our own circuit board.

Portobelo, Panama

Field tested.

In October 2026 we took Blackout to Portobelo, a UNESCO site in danger, with archaeologist Prof. Juan Ortega. It drove the fort, photographed the walls and identified bricks, coral stone and a glazed pottery shard on the spot.
