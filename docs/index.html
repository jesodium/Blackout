Recon run

Send it down a corridor or into a collapsed void on a routine that keeps running even if the link drops.

02

Live telemetry

Distance, temperature, humidity and heading stream back the whole way, alongside the camera feed.

The onboard voice names what the camera is looking at, out loud, in English or Spanish — no reading a screen in a hurry.

Findings log

Anything worth a second look is saved with its frame, so the run can be walked back through after the rover is out.
