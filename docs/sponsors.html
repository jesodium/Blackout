Sponsorship

Three ranks. One rover.

Blackout is built by students for WRO 2026 Future Innovators. Sponsors cover parts, batteries, print time and getting the rover to the venue. What you cover decides the rank, and the rank decides where your name shows up.

Diamond

Top rank. One or two names, no more, so it stays worth something.

  • Slot open
  • Logo on the rover chassis, where the camera sees it
  • Logo on the team shirts and the pit banner
  • Top billing on this site and in the project report
  • Named on stage in the WRO pitch and in every run video
  • A demo run for your people, rover and operator included

Platinum

The working rank — enough to keep a rebuild funded.

  • Slot open
  • Logo on the team shirts and the pit banner
  • Logo on this site and in the project report
  • Named in the run videos
  • Build updates before they go public

Standard

Parts, filament, a battery, a ride to the venue. All of it counts.

  • Slot open
  • Name on this site
  • Name in the project report and the credits
  • Build updates by email

Want a rank? Talk to the team on GitHub. Blackout is a school competition project, not a company — sponsorship pays for hardware and travel, and nothing here is a commercial offer.
